First-install notes

Three quick steps the first time you run it.

1

Click past the unsigned-app warning

This early build isn't code-signed yet, so your OS will warn you the first time. Windows: click More info → Run anyway. macOS: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, click Open anyway.

2

Sign in to your DSP portal

The app opens a browser window pointed at your DSP portal. Sign in with your normal SSO + MFA. RouteReady never sees or stores your password — Amazon collects it directly inside the app's browser.

3

That's it

The app saves an encrypted session to your machine and keeps RouteReady in sync from there. When the session expires, you'll get prompted to sign in again.
