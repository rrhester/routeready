Schedule

Driver performance

Workflows & checklists

Custom forms with GPS, signature, and photo capture. Operational assignment boards with driver-acknowledgment loops. SOP checklists with per-item attribution. Every workflow your DSP needs, signed in the field, on the driver's home-screen app.

Performance insights

Hiring & onboarding

From an Indeed upload to a signed-in driver — in one afternoon.

RouteReady runs the whole pipeline. Bulk-ingest applicants, auto-send screening links, run Interview Day on a single screen, and hand the new hire a phone they can sign into in one tap.

Bulk-ingest from Indeed

Paste your Indeed export and dozens of applicants land in your funnel in seconds — names, emails, phones, application dates, deduped against your existing pipeline. No copy-paste, no spreadsheet wrangling, no losing candidates between systems.

Auto-screen by SMS + email

The moment an applicant lands in your funnel, they get a personalized screening link. They record a 60-second intro video, answer your auto-fail questions, and book their own interview on your calendar — without you sending a single message.

Interview Day, one screen

Today's bookings stacked by time. Watch each applicant's intro video before they walk in. Hit Hired, No Hire, or No Show — the system records the outcome, sends the appropriate auto-response, and (on a hire) creates the driver record automatically.

Driver activates in one tap

The new hire gets a welcome email with their personal activation link. Tap it, pick a 4-digit PIN, and they're signed in — phone already on file, install instructions for the home-screen PWA already in their chat thread from dispatch.

Onboarding you actually control

Build the blueprint once: profile, license front and back, I-9 Section 1, handbook signature, training videos, payroll setup. The driver works through it on their phone; you see exactly which step every new hire is on, in real time.

I-9 + signed docs, built in

Federal Form I-9 Section 1 in the driver's app, Section 2 in yours — with the audit trail and document hash chain required to defend a re-verification. Custom NDAs, handbooks, drug-test consent: same envelope flow, same signed-record vault.

Compliance & defensibility

Court-grade by default. Silent until it isn't.

Amazon's DSP rules, FMCSA deadlines, federal I-9, and the full audit chain — enforced server-side, surfaced as one stoplight score, and quiet when there's nothing to act on. Most platforms drown operators in alerts. Ours speak only when you need to act.

Amazon 2-BD & 14-BD rules, enforced

Every grounded van shows two live countdowns — Open RO due (2 business days), Repair due (14 business days) — mapped directly to Amazon's named DSP rules. The compliance board flags them as separate exceptions so you can clear them one at a time, with a permanent audit row on each.

FMCSA MCS-150 from your USDOT

Tell us your USDOT. We'll tell you when your next biennial filing is due — computed from the digits, the way the federal rule actually works. Yellow at 60 days out. Red the day it lapses. Most owners don't realize it's deterministic. Now you do — and your operating authority doesn't quietly expire.

Grounded vans can't be assigned

Flip a van to grounded. Every same-day-and-future assignment to that van is deleted — with an audit row on each. Your dispatch board cannot lie about which van is drivable. The most expensive operational mistake at a DSP, structurally impossible.

Hash-chained audit, everywhere

Documents, assignments, compliance events, coaching edits — every meaningful action is appended to a SHA-256 chain. Re-walkable. Independently verifiable. The packet you hand an adjudicator, an ICE auditor, or an unemployment hearing is the packet that was true on the day.

Closed-loop attendance & coaching

Click Approve on a no-show. The system stamps the shift status, evaluates your policy ladder, drafts the coaching record at the right severity, sends it to the driver's task feed, and waits for the signed acknowledgment. One click. Documented. Defensible. Every driver, every time, the same way.

Compliance noise that isn't

"Driver has no van" doesn't fire if there's an auto-assignable van in the pool. Snoozed exceptions log a permanent audit row and re-fire after 14 days. The board stays quiet until it isn't — so when it lights up, you act. Alert fatigue is why most platforms fail. Ours is silent until it isn't.
