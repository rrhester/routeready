Features

Everything an Amazon DSP needs — in one place.

Twelve capabilities that replace six tools and a stack of spreadsheets. Designed around the actual workflow of operating a DSP, not generic HR software bolted onto delivery.

Hiring Pipeline

Applicants apply on Indeed. RouteReady screens via SMS, scores them automatically, and books interviews on Cal.com. Interview Day mode gives dispatchers one focused screen.

Indeed · SMS · Cal.com

Performance Management

Score-based accountability with staffing-risk-aware termination ceilings. Bottom-N selector. Suggested action per driver — coach, warn, suspend, or term — capped by your current bench position.

Bottom-N · Risk-aware

Attendance Tracking

Mark drivers Present, Late, Callout, No-show, or VTO from one screen. Configurable points-or-occurrence policy. VTO never counts against the driver. Exempt categories for PTO, jury duty, FMLA.

Points · Occurrence · Hybrid

OKAMI Daily Planning

13-week strategic capacity plan plus daily route targets for the next 3 weeks. Set cushion as % or headcount. System recommends a cushion based on your callout and VTO rates — and drives every shift count downstream.

13-week · Daily · Cushion

Smart Scheduling

Score-aware driver pool, lineup-quality KPI per day, conflict markers, swap requests. Top performers surface first; bottom performers get VTO offered first. Score never blocks scheduling — only orders positive choices.

Score-aware · Lineup KPI

License & DOT Compliance

Auto-text drivers 30 and 14 days before license expires. Schedule view flags drivers expiring this week. Auto-block past-expiry scheduling. Each reminder lands in the driver's HR file as a permanent record.

Auto-SMS · Schedule flag

Driver HR File

Every suspension, written warning, and reinstatement becomes a timestamped, immutable record. Defensible documentation when termination is challenged or unemployment is contested. Export as a single PDF on demand.

Immutable · Exportable

Coaching Drawer

One drawer to coach by SMS, log a 1:1, pull a driver from a route, or initiate a structured suspension with reason category and evidence. Every action lands in the driver's record automatically.

SMS · Suspend · 1:1

Daily Inspections

Pre-trip and post-trip checklists with photo and video upload. Compliance evidence stored alongside each shift. GPS-triggered prompts on return so EOD steps don't get skipped.

Photo · Video · GPS

Fleet & Assets

Vehicles, phones, fuel cards, toll tags, key fobs. Assignment history per asset, document storage with expiry reminders, maintenance tracking. Every issued asset traceable back to the driver who has it.

Vehicles · Documents

Invoice Reconciliation

Reconcile your Variable invoice line-by-line against your own delivery records. Auto-draft dispute letters with evidence attached. Track recovery in one place. The math runs nightly so disputes are ready when you are.

Auto-dispute · Recovery

Smart Drop Ingestion

Drop any messy export — payroll CSV, Mentor scorecard, dispatch sheet, 47-column ADP file. RouteReady extracts, maps to schema, and routes the data into the right tool. No more manual cleanup.

CSV · PDF · Excel

FAQs

Common questions about RouteReady.

Find answers to the most common questions about how the platform works and how DSPs get started.

What is RouteReady, and how does it work?
RouteReady is the operations platform for Amazon DSPs. It replaces the spreadsheets, group chats, and standalone tools most DSPs cobble together for hiring, scheduling, performance management, and compliance. Everything lives in one workspace — connected by shared driver records and HR files.
Do I have to give you access to my Amazon Logistics Portal?
No. RouteReady runs on data you already control — your driver list, your scorecards, your invoices. You upload or sync those into RouteReady. We don't need (and don't ask for) Amazon Logistics Portal credentials. Some integrations are coming as Amazon expands its partner program.
Do I have to do extra manual data entry?
No. Smart Drop ingestion takes the messy exports you already have — payroll CSVs, Mentor scorecards, dispatch sheets, even 47-column ADP files — and maps them into the right tools automatically. The morning check-in is the only daily action, and it's one tap per driver.
How quickly can I get started?
Most DSPs are operating in RouteReady by their second cycle. Launch involves importing your driver list, setting your attendance policy, and connecting Indeed for hiring. Our team walks you through every step — most operations are live in under a week.
Is my data secure?
Yes. RouteReady runs on enterprise infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit. HR files are immutable — once a record is created, it can be voided with a reason but never silently deleted. Each DSP's data is logically isolated. Full audit trail on every change.
What kind of support do you provide?
Real humans, fast response. Email support@gorouteready.com or use the in-app chat. We watch every account during the first month — you won't be left to figure things out alone. Optional add-on services (hiring support, payroll, invoice audits) come with dedicated specialists.
Can I try RouteReady before committing?
Yes. Schedule a demo and we'll walk you through the platform with sample data tailored to a DSP your size. No pressure, no contract — and the platform is month-to-month, so if it doesn't fit you can cancel after one cycle.
Can I cancel my service?
Anytime, no penalty. RouteReady is month-to-month. Email support@gorouteready.com and we'll close your account at the end of the current billing cycle. You can export all your data — driver records, HR files, attendance logs — before account closure.
How do I request deletion of my data?
Email support@gorouteready.com with the request. We'll confirm the request, export any data you want to retain, and permanently delete your records within 30 days. Some records may be retained longer if required by law (e.g., HR files subject to wage-hour regulations).
Do you support multiple stations?
Yes. RouteReady supports multi-station DSPs out of the box. Each station has its own roster, schedule, and attendance settings, but rolls up to one workspace for owner-level views. Add a station from settings — no extra license needed.
